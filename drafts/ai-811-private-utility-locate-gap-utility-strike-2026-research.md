# Research: The 811 Gap — Why Calling Before You Dig Still Isn't Enough

Slug: `ai-811-private-utility-locate-gap-utility-strike-2026`
Journalist: Frank "The Foreman" DeLuca (Project Management & Operations)
Date: October 4, 2026

## Angle
Every residential dig (ADU foundation, pool, sewer lateral, fence, deck footings) risks a utility strike. Calling 811 is free and legally required, but it only marks utility-owned lines up to the meter/demarcation point. Everything on the private side (house to ADU, gas to fire pit, irrigation, septic, private lighting) is the property owner's problem, and nobody marks it unless you pay someone to. Meanwhile locator errors account for nearly a quarter of all strikes, so even a perfect 811 ticket leaves residual risk. AI multi-sensor mapping (Exodigo) is the emerging fix for the "nobody knows what's down there" problem.

## Kill test
Helps anyone building or buying a home: concrete pre-excavation checklist with sourced costs (free 811 ticket + $250-500 private locate + hand-dig in the 24-inch tolerance zone + pothole verification). Passes.

### 5. The 811 private-line gap + California law
- 811 notifies member utilities only; locators mark utility-owned facilities up to the meter/demarcation point.
- NOT marked: power house-to-ADU/garage/pool, water after the meter, irrigation, gas meter-to-BBQ/fire pit/pool heater, private sewer laterals beyond utility responsibility, septic, private lighting/fiber/security.
- Property owner owns and is responsible for protecting private underground utilities; private locating is a paid service.
- California Government Code 4216: excavators (including homeowners with power equipment) must notify 811; digging without a ticket and hitting a line = liable for repair costs, civil penalties, resulting damage. "Didn't know" is not a defense.
- Tolerance zone = 24 inches each side of the mark (4216(u)). Within the zone, 4216.4 requires hand-exposing to determine exact location before power equipment or boring; vacuum excavation allowed with operator agreement; presume all lines active; call 911 on gas/hazardous-liquid damage.
- Sources: dpcoftexas.org homeowner education; codes.findlaw.com (Gov 4216); law.onecle.com (4216.4); carcga.org.

### 6. Private locating cost benchmarks
- Residential flat/minimum: $250-500 basic scan on standard lot; hourly $150-300/hr with 1-2 hr minimum (engineerfix.com).
- Small residential (e.g., pool): $200-500; medium commercial $500-1,000 (aeanet.org).
- Penhall (private locating provider) cites CGA: $4.62 saved per $1 spent on damage prevention.
- Orange County ranges: trip minimums $350-600 residential; GPR surveys cost more than EM-only locates.
- Who pays: property owner/GC/excavator; on pro jobs it sits in the preconstruction budget.

## Original contribution (the math)
The article's novel framing is the **three-layer dig plan** with sourced unit economics:
1. **Free layer (811 ticket):** $0, legally required, covers only public-side lines to the meter. Still leaves 22.3% root cause (no notification) on the table if skipped, and 23.4% locator-error risk even if done.
2. **Paid layer (private locate):** ~$250-500 residential flat scan; covers the ~60% of buried infrastructure that is privately owned (house-to-ADU gas, irrigation, septic, private electric). CGA: $4.62 saved per $1 spent.
3. **Discipline layer (tolerance zone):** hand-dig/pothole within 24" of marks (CA law); 14.8% of strikes come from failing to maintain clearance after marks are verified.
- Strike cost stack: PG&E average $3,500 direct repair bill + downstream 16:1 ratio (CGA) + CA civil penalties + schedule delay (crew idle, re-mobilization) + worst case: injury (gas strikes = 911, evacuation, fire department response).
- Break-even: one avoided strike pays for roughly 7 private locates at $500 each against the $3,500 average repair alone, before downstream costs.
- Skepticism: Exodigo's 99% claim is vendor-reported, unverified independently, and priced for infrastructure-scale projects, not $500 residential scans; traditional EM + GPR from a competent private locator is the realistic residential tool. 811 marks expire (CA: ticket valid window; renew if schedule slips). Marks degrade; white-line your dig area.
