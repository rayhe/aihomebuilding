# Research: AI Title Insurance Automation Cost Gap

## Core Angle
Title insurance is a $27B industry that pays out only 5% of premiums in claims. AI is automating 80% of searches. Homebuyers are still paying $2,000+ per transaction for a product whose risk model AI just proved was wildly overpriced.

## Key Data Points

### Industry Economics
- Title insurance pays only 5% of premiums in claims — vs 70%+ for other insurance types (Urban Institute)
- If title insurance were priced like other insurance, premiums could be <0.05% of home price — less than 1/10 current rates (Urban Institute)
- $600-900B in risk exposure mitigated annually (First American, June 2026)
- Industry has only a few large insurers (Fidelity National, First American, Old Republic, Stewart)
- Costs scale poorly: much lower share of sales price for expensive homes → fixed/admin cost-driven

### AI Disruption
- Doma (proptech): AI-powered instant title decisioning
- "Upfront Title" product: saves borrowers 40-70% vs traditional rates
- 80% of refinance candidates qualified for title insurance waiver in Fannie Mae pilot
- Fannie Mae pilot extended through 2027
- Opendoor acquired part of Doma (April 2026) — 85 Doma employees joined Opendoor
- Blend partnership: embedded instant title into borrower application flow

### Industry Pushback
- First American (June 2026 white paper): "AI alone cannot meet the industry's standards for accuracy, consistency and reliability"
- Claims that "low claims are a feature, not a bug" — prevention-based model
- ALTA research: 40%+ of refinance losses linked to fraud/forgery (off-record risks)
- Average claim cost exceeds $200,000
- HousingWire (Feb 2026): "Coverage decisions should never be based solely on automated review"
- Off-record risks: fraud, forgery, identity misrepresentation — not in public records

### Consumer Impact
- Average homebuyer pays ~$1,000-3,000 for title insurance
- Sold through agents with lender/realtor commissions — consumers have little visibility
- Title searches can take days; AI does them in seconds/minutes
- Most homebuyers don't shop for title insurance — lender/realtor referral pipeline
