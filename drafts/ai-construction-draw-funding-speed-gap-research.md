# Research: AI Construction Loan Draw Funding Speed Gap

## Topic
The gap between AI-powered construction loan draw processing (3 minutes) and the industry standard (7 business days). Construction draws are the lifeblood of custom home building — every day a draw is delayed costs the builder carrying costs and delays the project. AI tools exist that cut this from weeks to minutes, but adoption among residential lenders is minimal.

## Journalist
Frank "The Foreman" DeLuca — Project Management & Operations

## Kill Test
✅ Directly affects anyone building a custom home. Draw delays = construction delays = cost overruns. If you're a builder or homeowner-builder, knowing about AI draw processing could save you weeks and thousands of dollars.

## Core Thesis
Construction loan draws — the periodic funding releases that keep a home build moving — take an average of 7 business days to process. The manual review alone takes 20-40 hours of human labor per draw. An AI agent now processes them in 3 minutes with 99%+ accuracy and catches 4x more risks than human reviewers. But most residential lenders are still using email, spreadsheets, and a guy who drives to the job site. The gap between what's possible and what's happening is costing builders and homeowners real money.

## Cost Impact Analysis (Original Calculation)
- Average custom home build: $400,000-$600,000
- Typical construction loan rate: 8-10% (2026, variable)
- Average build timeline: 8-12 months
- Typical number of draws: 4-6 during construction
- Daily carrying cost on $500K at 9%: ~$123/day
- If each draw takes 7 business days (9 calendar days) vs. 3 business days (4 calendar days):
  - Savings per draw: 5 calendar days × $123 = $615
  - Over 5 draws: $3,075 in reduced carrying costs
  - Plus: reduced risk of subcontractor payment delays → fewer liens → fewer project stoppages
  - Plus: faster project completion → earlier occupancy or sale

## Strongest Counterargument
Physical inspections serve a purpose that photos can't fully replace. An inspector on site can:
- Check behind walls, under structures
- Assess quality of work subjectively
- Catch issues that aren't visible in photos
- Verify materials match specifications
- The 400% increase in "risks detected" by AI may include false positives that waste time
- Construction fraud (inflating completion percentage) is a real problem, and trusting borrower-submitted photos has obvious moral hazard

## Limitations
- Built Technologies' data is self-reported (no independent audit)
- The 99%+ accuracy claim is across their platform, not independently verified
- Most data comes from commercial construction, not residential custom homes
- Small/community banks that typically fund custom home construction loans may lack IT infrastructure for these tools
- The Truepic/Cotality partnership was announced June 2026 — too new for outcomes data
- We couldn't verify adoption rates among residential construction lenders specifically
- Carrying cost calculation assumes variable-rate construction loan (common but not universal)
