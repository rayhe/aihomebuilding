## Topic Summary
Terran Robotics (Bloomington, IN HQ, build site Luling/Lockhart TX) built first home April 27, 2026 at Proto-Town using cable-driven AI robot "Terry" that autonomously picks, places, and hammers monolithic adobe (clay, soil, water, straw) sourced on-site. Zero-mile materials, 80% less embodied carbon than conventional, fireproof/mold-free/soundproof/zero-VOC, thermal mass walls replacing framing+insulation+siding+drywall. Code-compliant via IRC Appendix U — Cob Construction (Monolithic Adobe), first approved for 2021 IRC (93-6 ICC vote Oct 2019, online 2/3 approval Dec 2019). Robot: 4-tower cable gantry, football-field workspace, 2-person 1-day setup from truck/trailer, 10-lb increments, depth cameras + reinforcement learning + computer vision for real-time void detection and correction. Operates 24/7. Backed by NSF grant (~$250K), Elevate Ventures, Flywheel Fund, partnerships Autodesk + ARUP (Sydney Opera House engineers). Planned 20+ units at Proto-Town next year, ADUs in Bloomington IN, pocket neighborhoods in Columbus IN with Chestnut Development.

Connects to HUD funding: HUD PDR-2600-DC-029Q "Mass Market Solutions for Leveraging Robotics and AI Technologies for Home Construction Demonstration" — up to $10M awards, $3M min, due July 13 2026, plus $3M automated permitting demo PDR-2600-DC-029O. Federal signal that robotics + AI for housing is policy priority.

## Original Contribution Angles
1. **Cost math nobody did:** Conventional wall assembly cost breakdown: framing ($12-18/sq ft wall), insulation ($2-4), siding ($6-12), drywall ($2.5-3.5) = $22.5-37.5/sq ft wall vs adobe material $0 + robot amortized cost. Even at $13.5K-$25K robot cost (Unitree G1 scale reference), break-even at ~500-1000 sq ft wall area. Terran claims "cheapest material" — need to quantify: excavation spoil reuse saves $2K-$4K dump fees + $3K-$6K material purchase.
2. **Code adoption gap:** IRC Appendix U is optional — how many jurisdictions have adopted it as of 2026? Search showed strawbale adopted by 6 states/9 localities, but cob adoption count not published. Need to check Texas (unincorporated Travis/Caldwell counties where Proto-Town sits) — Texas has no statewide residential code, county adoption is minimal, enabling experimentation. This explains why first home is in Luling TX not California.
3. **Embodied carbon verification:** 80% less claim — cross-check with conventional stick-frame embodied carbon ~30-50 kgCO2e/sq ft vs adobe ~5-10 kgCO2e/sq ft (literature). Need to flag: does 80% include foundation? Terran uses helical piles or conventional foundation still needed? Original article says zero steel but foundation still required. Calculate true whole-home carbon reduction likely 40-55% not 80% when including roof, MEP, foundation.
4. **Reinforcement learning novelty:** Unlike pre-programmed robots (FBR Hadrian X 360 blocks/hr, SAM100), Terry is RL-based — improves daily. This is first construction robot described as "constantly using cameras to understand world and react" vs pre-programmed toolpaths. Quantify: depth camera topographical heat maps, void detection, next-layer compensation if wall settles overnight.

## Skepticism / Counterarguments
- Corners stump them now (WebProNews) — first home mixed 2 earthen + wood frame, next planned all-earth but corners remain engineering challenge for monolithic cob (cold joints, cracking).
- IRC Appendix U optional — most jurisdictions have NOT adopted, so permits difficult/impossible in most US. Texas advantage due to no statewide code.
- 80% embodied carbon claim is walls only, not whole home. Roof, foundation, windows, MEP still conventional carbon. Whole-home reduction smaller.
- Moisture: adobe requires stucco coating + moisture barrier detailing (Texas clay helps per Nikoulina dampness note but still needs protection). Not suitable for wet climates without modifications.
- Scalability: 20 units planned vs 4.7M shortage (Zillow) — drop in bucket. Robot covers football-field workspace but needs 4 towers + earth screws — site prep time not included in 2-person 1-day claim.
- HUD funding is $10M miniscule vs $5.4B invested in contech 2020-2024 — signal not scale.
