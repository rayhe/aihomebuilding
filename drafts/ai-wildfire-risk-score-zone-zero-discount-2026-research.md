# Research Notes: AI Is Scoring Your Home's Wildfire Risk at the Property Level — and California Just Made the 5-Foot Perimeter Law

**Slug:** ai-wildfire-risk-score-zone-zero-discount-2026
**Article #:** 913
**Journalist:** Priya Greenwood (sustainability & green building)
**Date:** September 19, 2026

## Angle
Insurers have stopped rating wildfire risk by ZIP code. They now score the specific five feet around your house from aerial imagery, and the same perimeter just became state law (Zone 0, adopted Aug 19, 2026). The article combines IBHS's cost study with California's actual discount numbers to compute a real payback — and exposes that two neighbors doing identical hardening can get wildly different discounts because California lets each insurer set its own.

## Primary Sources

### 1. IBHS + Headwaters Economics, "Construction Costs for Wildfire-Resistant Homes" (Oct 14, 2025)
- 1,750 sq ft single-family home, Altadena CA, $500,000 estimated construction cost
- Building to IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home Plus adds ~$15,000 vs traditional methods (less than 3% of the cost of the wildfire-relevant components: roofing, siding, windows)
- Range across standards (CA WUI code minimum, WFPH Base, WFPH Plus): ~$9,000 to $15,000
- Component details: fire-safe roof alternatives differ by less than $1,000 vs a ~$25,000 conventional roof; IBHS Plus eaves at $5,200 are nearly 3x the non-standard alternative
- Steve Hawkes (IBHS wildfire director): "$50 extra per month on a traditional 30-year mortgage"
- Context: Jan 2025 LA wildfires destroyed 16,000+ structures, $250B+ economic losses; wildfire standards required in Palisades, not throughout Altadena recovery area
- URL: https://ibhs.org/ibhs-news-releases/new-headwaters-ibhs-study-finds-adding-greater-wildfire-resilience-to-new-construction-in-altadena-affordable/

### 2. IBHS Wildfire Policy page (ibhs.org/wildfire-policy)
- The 0-5 Foot Noncombustible Zone (Zone 0) "may be the single-most effective mitigation a state can enforce to reduce risk at scale"
- Building to IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home Base (which exceeds current CA WUI code) adds less than $3,000 to total construction costs
- States with modern statewide WUI codes: California (CBC Part 7 / Chapter 7A) and Utah (2003 IWUIC)
- HOA rules can undermine mitigation (appearance standards vs wildfire practices)
- URL: https://ibhs.org/wildfire-policy/

### 3. California Zone 0 regulations — adopted Aug 19, 2026 (Board of Forestry and Fire Protection, 8-0 vote)
- First-in-the-nation rules; applies to ~2 million structures in highest-risk areas
- Final rules weaker than earlier proposals: non-combustible zone reduced from full 5 feet to a minimum of 1 foot; some plants/grass allowed in remainder; trees OK if limbs trimmed away from ground and roof; wood gates/fences within 5 ft must be replaced with non-flammable versions
- Effective after OAL review (expected ~Sep 2026 for new construction); existing homes phase in over 3-5 years
- Legal basis: AB 3074 (2020), SB 504 (2024), AB 1455 (2025) amending PRC 4291; Newsom EO N-18-25 (Feb 2025)
- Fire scientist pushback: battalion chief Todd Lando (Central Marin FD) advocated for fully non-combustible Zone 0 — "the science supports it"; weaker rules harder to enforce
- Insurance-crisis context: ~60,000 homes destroyed in last decade; hundreds of thousands forced onto expensive FAIR Plan coverage
- URLs: https://www.eenews.net/articles/california-approves-landmark-zone-zero-wildfire-rules/ ; https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/west/2026/08/20/882337.htm

### 4. California Department of Insurance — Safer from Wildfires regulation (CCR § 2644.9, effective Oct 2022; mandatory discounts since April 2023)
- 100% of CA insurance market required to offer wildfire safety discounts (up from 7% when Lara took office in 2019)
- Each insurer sets its OWN discounts (unlike Alabama wind program which sets uniform ranges) — creates large inconsistencies
- FAIR Plan Dwelling Fire policies (policies effective 11/15/2025+): up to 12 discounts; all 12 = up to 16.4% off the wildfire portion of premium; earlier version: 14.5% for Immediate Surroundings + Structure combined; 5% for Immediate Surroundings alone (cleared 5 ft, no combustible sheds within 30 ft, PRC 4291 compliance, ember-resistant 5-ft zone); FAIR Plan seeking 35.8% average rate hike (applied April 2026), but up to 15% discounts available for mitigation
- State Farm (approved filing): 6.3% total for all Safer from Wildfires standards + Firewise community = avg $113/yr savings; 7 of 10 individual standards worth only 0.1% each; IBHS certification adds 2-3.8% but requires a $125 inspection fee that wipes out first-year savings (Consumer Watchdog)
- General Insurance Co. (Liberty Mutual): up to 21% off wildfire portion with community discounts + IBHS cert; Angels Camp example: $286/yr on $2,202 premium; Pasadena example: same 21% = $5 on $3,573 premium (risk-based). State Farm all-12-steps: high-risk property $13,788 premium cut by $1,441/yr
- URLs: https://www.eenews.net/articles/california-insurers-begin-giving-discounts-for-fire-proofed-homes/ ; https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2024/05/21/262333.htm ; https://www.cfpnet.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Wildfire-Hardening-Discounts-for-Dwelling-Fire-Commercial-Policies-2025.11.15.pdf

### 5. AI property-level wildfire scoring
- Zesty.ai (Bay Area startup): property-level risk scoring from building info + aerial imagery + decades of wildfire data + fire-scientist data; partnered with MetLife
- Cape Analytics: AI + geospatial imagery for defensible-space/vulnerability assessment at US-wide property scale
- Liberty + ICEYE: building-level parametric wildfire product using Synthetic Aperture Radar (sees through smoke, night, all weather); ML classifies each insured property as destroyed/undamaged within hours; payouts triggered in days
- NEW (2026) academic manuscript: drone/UAV imagery (1-4 cm ground sampling distance) + multimodal visual learning models to detect small policy-relevant Zone 0 hazards: trash cans, propane tanks, vehicles, wood piles — objects insurers' satellite imagery cannot resolve. Cites: Hedayati et al. 2018 (IBHS near-building noncombustible zone report); Zamanialaei et al. 2025 (Nature Communications, combustible materials in 5-ft perimeter are among strongest ignition predictors); CA PRC 4291; IBHS WFPH technical standard
- URL: https://github.com/basanthpr/hiz-wildfire-assessment/blob/HEAD/AI%20and%20HIZ_Manuscript.docx.md ; https://uphelp.org/1-big-thing-insurance-deploying-ai-against-wildfires/?print=print

### 6. CA 2025 WUI Code (CBC Chapter 7A, effective Jan 1, 2026)
- Prohibits cedar shakes and wood shingles in WUI zones and Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones regardless of fire-retardant treatment
- Formalizes Zone 0 ember-resistant perimeter requirement; ignition-resistant material standards for roofing, siding, vents

## Original Contribution (novel calculation)
Payback math combining sources 1 + 4:
- WFPH Plus hardening on a $500K new build: ~$15,000 added cost
- High-risk property with all 12 Safer-from-Wildfires steps + IBHS cert (State Farm example): $13,788 premium reduced by $1,441/year → premium-only payback ≈ 10.4 years
- BUT the cheapest fixes pay back in under a year: IBHS WFPH Base (exceeds CA WUI code) costs less than $3,000; the Zone 0-adjacent discount tier (Immediate Surroundings, FAIR Plan: 5% on wildfire portion) rewards nearly free labor (clearing debris, moving firewood, replacing mulch)
- The distortion: the same 21% discount = $286/yr in Angels Camp vs $5/yr in Pasadena — discount dollars flow where risk is highest, meaning hardening payback is BEST exactly where fires are worst
- Contrast gap nobody calculated: insurers' AI can now resolve a trash can in your 5-foot perimeter (drone VLM study) — meaning the "dumb" compliance step (moving a garbage can) is being graded at 1-4 cm resolution while the $5,200 eaves upgrade gets the same checkbox

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? Yes:
- Rebuilding after fire (Altadena/Palisades) or building new in WUI: 3% marginal cost to build fire-ready — the single cheapest insurance decision in the build budget
- Existing homeowner in CA high-risk zone: Zone 0 compliance is now law with a 3-5 year phase-in; the discounts they can demand have dollar values attached
- Homebuyer: AI scoring means the listing's roof material and 5-foot perimeter affect insurability and price, not just the view

## Counterargument (strongest)
Zone 0 as adopted is weaker than the science: 1-foot minimum noncombustible vs the 5-foot noncombustible zone IBHS research says is "the single-most effective mitigation." A 2026 homeowner could comply fully with state law and still sit in the ember-ignition band the insurers' models penalize. Also, discount arithmetic is cruel at the bottom: Pasadena's 21% discount worth $5/year shows compliance costs can exceed premium savings where risk is low — the regulation mandates the offer, not that it pencils out. And AI scoring cuts both ways: the same aerial models that reward hardening will non-renew you for a wood fence you didn't know was graded.

## Limitations
- IBHS cost study is one 1,750 sq ft archetype in Altadena; costs scale with roof complexity, siding area, and regional labor (union markets narrower gap). No third-party audit of per-unit costs on multi-home projects.
- Premium savings examples are carrier-specific illustrations (State Farm, Liberty Mutual/General Insurance, FAIR Plan) — each insurer's filing differs; quotes vary by wildfire score class.
- Zone 0 final text pending OAL filing; local governments may craft their own rules (variance risk).
- Drone-VLM Zone 0 hazard detection is a research manuscript, not a deployed insurer product — satellite imagery insurers use today lacks the 0-5 ft resolution it promises.
