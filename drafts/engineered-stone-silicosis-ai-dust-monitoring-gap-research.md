# Research: Engineered Stone Countertops, Silicosis, and the AI Dust Monitoring Gap

## Angle
Your quartz countertop is 93% crystalline silica. The workers who cut it are developing fatal lung disease at epidemic rates. Real-time AI-powered dust monitors exist and cost a fraction of one workers' comp claim. Most fabrication shops — 80% have fewer than 10 employees — don't use them.

## Journalist: Catherine Chen (Policy & Regulation)
Policy/regulation story at heart: OSHA revising silica rules, WOEMA petitioning for an outright ban, Congressional legislation pending, $200M+ in litigation.

## Primary Sources

### California CDPH Data
- **519 confirmed cases** of engineered-stone-associated silicosis since 2019
- **29 deaths** since 2019
- Median age at diagnosis: **46**; median age at death: **49**
- 25 (48%) continued to fabricate stone AFTER being diagnosed with silicosis
- 42 (82%) had 1 or more respiratory-related emergency department visits
- 10 (19%) fatal; 6 (12%) alive with chronic resting hypoxia
- 11 referred for lung transplant: 3 underwent transplant (1 died), 7 declined transplant (6 died)
- Median work tenure: **15 years** (IQR 10-20)
- Source: Fazio et al., JAMA (2023); CDPH ongoing surveillance

### CDPH Business Survey
- Identified **1,342 fabrication businesses** in California using NAICS/SIC codes
- Of those, **653 confirmed fabricators** (480 have <10 employees, 291 have ≤5)
- **51% of inspected shops** had at least one employee exposed above OSHA PEL of 50 µg/m³
- More than **half** of identified shops have at least one confirmed silicosis case
- Source: CDC/NIOSH 2026 bulletin

### OSHA Regulatory Data
- Current PEL: **50 µg/m³** (reduced 80% from old limit of 250 µg/m³ in 2016)
- Table 1: 18 approved control methods for limiting silica dust
- OSHA is actively seeking data to **revise Table 1** (RIN:1218-AD18)
- OSHA estimated annual implementation cost: **$1.03 billion**
- Industry groups argued costs were underestimated by **up to 10x**
- OSHA estimated the rule would prevent **642 deaths and 918 moderate-to-severe lung disease cases annually**
- Rule upheld by DC Circuit in December 2017
- Source: Bloomberg Law; OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1153

### 2026 NIOSH/OSHA Hazard Alert
- New 2026 guidance specifically for countertop manufacturing/finishing/installation
- Recommends hierarchy of controls
- Source: CDC Safe Work Practices page, updated 2026

### WOEMA Petition (December 2025)
- Western Occupational and Environmental Medicine Association (600+ occupational medicine physicians)
- Petitioned Cal/OSHA Standards Board for **immediate prohibition** on artificial stone containing >1% crystalline silica
- Cited data showing silicosis cases **continue to emerge even in compliant shops** (wet cutting, ventilation, PPE)
- Source: Stone World

### Australia Ban
- Australia imposed a **full ban** on engineered stone in mid-2024
- First country to do so
- Source: Multiple

### Material Composition
- Engineered stone: **>90% crystalline silica** (some up to 95%)
- Natural granite: **<45% silica**
- Quartz surface imports to US increased **~800%** from 2010 to 2018
- Also contains heavy metals, resins, glues releasing toxic VOCs when fabricated
- Source: MMWR 2019; Brayton Purcell

### Litigation
- **$200 million** in verdicts/settlements secured by Brayton Purcell alone (as of Feb 2026)
- **$52.4 million** verdict: Gonzalez v. Caesarstone USA (Aug 2024, LA County) — 34-year-old needed double lung transplant
- **$26 million+** confidential settlement (Apr 2025) — 51-year-old, oxygen-dependent
- **$17.45 million** verdict: Colorado (May 2026)
- **370+ individual lawsuits** filed against manufacturers
- Brayton Purcell currently represents **700+ clients**
- Average pre-trial settlement: **$300K-$500K**; trial verdict: **$1M-$10M+**
- No class action yet, but MDL consolidation pressure growing
- Congressional legislation pending that would **shield manufacturers from liability**
- Source: PR Newswire, Stone World, KFF Health News

### KFF Health News Investigation
- David Michaels (former OSHA head under Obama): "This is comparable to the tobacco industry saying cigarettes are safe"
- Cases appearing from CA to TX, FL, and Northeast
- Not nationally reportable disease — no comprehensive count exists
- Workers often Hispanic men in 30s-40s
- Source: KFF Health News

## AI / Technology Angle

### Existing Dust Monitoring Technology
- **FieldProxy**: AI-powered dust containment monitoring automation for construction/flooring
  - IoT sensors trigger when particulate levels exceed OSHA PEL
  - Immediate SMS/app alerts to supervisors
  - Photo evidence, timestamped logs, compliance checklists
  - Exportable OSHA documentation reports
  - Works across multiple concurrent jobsites
- **UNL research prototype**: Network of low-cost mobile dust sensors with BIM visualization
  - Single-board computers + dust sensors
  - Automated sensor calibration against ground truth
  - Real-time visualization in BIM model
  - Source: University of Nebraska-Lincoln
- **Low-cost particulate sensors**: $150-300 per unit for PM2.5/PM10 monitors
- **MEMS sensor costs**: Sub-$1 per accelerometer (from D.R. Horton article); similar decline in particulate sensors

### The Gap
- 480 of 653 California fabricators have <10 employees
- Most small shops can't afford dedicated safety officers
- Real-time monitoring would catch exposure before it becomes dangerous
- But no fabrication-specific AI monitoring tool exists at scale
- Commercial tools (FieldProxy) focus on large construction/flooring, not small stone fab shops
- The cost of one sensor network (~$1,000-$5,000) vs. one lawsuit ($300K-$52M)
