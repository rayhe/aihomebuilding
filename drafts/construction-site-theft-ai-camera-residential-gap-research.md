# Research: Construction Site Theft — The AI Camera Gap for Residential Builders

## Thesis
The U.S. construction industry loses $300 million to $1 billion annually to site theft. AI-powered security cameras and monitoring services now exist — but they're priced for commercial projects at $500-$2,000/month. Meanwhile, consumer-grade AI cameras (Wyze, Ring, eufy) with person detection, solar power, and 4G LTE connectivity cost $60-$100 per unit and work without WiFi. Nobody has packaged them for residential builders, and most residential builders don't deploy any security at all. The math is obvious: three $80 cameras pay for themselves with a single prevented theft.

## Key Sources & Data

### Financial losses
- **$300M-$1B annual losses** from construction site theft in the US (National Insurance Crime Bureau + National Equipment Register)
- **$300M specifically for residential** construction equipment theft (NER estimate, via RBT CPAs analysis)
- **$400M** most common estimate for equipment theft alone, excluding materials, tools, fuel (ECAM/industry reporting)
- **$2,500 average replacement cost** per small theft incident (ECAM industry data)
- **$10,000/day** average delay cost when theft/vandalism halts progress (ECAM)
- **2% of gross revenues** — estimated annual "shrink" on home building sites (Richard Rowerdink, "Security and Material Controls on the Job Site")
- Construction shrinkage = $1 billion minimum per year in materials alone (NAHB/Freedman estimate)

### Prevalence & patterns
- **11,000+ theft incidents** reported to FBI in 2021 — surpassed convenience store thefts (FBI data)
- **70% of construction workers** witness theft on sites they work on (BauWatch 2024 Construction Crime Index, survey of 500 UK workers)
- **85% of job site thefts go unreported** — below insurance deductibles or no evidence (industry estimate via getsafeandsound.com)
- **31% of projects** delayed by construction crime (BauWatch 2024)
- **64% say theft worse** than 5 years ago, rising to 88% for experienced workers (SafeSite Facilities/FM Business Daily survey, Nov 2025)
- **62% of residential site managers** concerned about theft (SafeSite survey)
- **30% of workers say security is "low priority"** on their sites (BauWatch 2024)
- **Recovery rate: <25%**, some studies show 7% (NICB/NER)
- **One-third of projects** experience delays directly from criminal activity

### What gets stolen (BauWatch 2024 breakdown)
- Small tools: most common (easy to steal, sell discreetly)
- Timber: 22%
- Pipework/ducting: 22%
- Bricks, blocks, roofing tiles: 26%
- Personal effects (phones, PPE): 20%
- Household appliances (boilers, fixtures): 19%
- Heavy machinery: only 12% (less common than expected)
- Vehicles: 12%
- In 64% of incidents, stolen items total £500-£4,000 ($630-$5,040)
- Only 6% exceed £10,000 ($12,600) per incident

### Commercial AI security solutions (priced for big jobs)
- **ECAM**: Live video monitoring + AI detection, mobile surveillance units, $1,000-$2,000+/month. UL-listed, TMA Five Diamond. 99% detection accuracy, 99% system uptime. Nationwide 140+ field technicians.
- **Ring Jobsite Security**: Mobile Security Trailer starting at $4,999. 360° Elite camera with Retinal 4K. 12' collapsible mast. Solar off-grid option. AI Active Warnings with two-way audio. Optional Virtual Security Guard ($).
- **Milesight**: Construction CCTV mobile surveillance units (Trailer, Tower, Standalone). 4G/5G, solar-powered. AI PPE compliance + intruder detection. Enterprise pricing.
- **Avigilon (Motorola)**: AI-powered analytics + construction camera monitoring. Enterprise-grade, commercial pricing.
- **LVT**: Mobile security units. Case study: Aspect Homes (residential developer) — 50% decrease in trespassing, zero thefts on sites with LVT units. Enterprise pricing.

### Consumer AI cameras (the missing residential solution)
- **Wyze Solar Cam Pan**: $80. 2K, 360° pan-tilt, AI person detection, solar powered, no wiring. $15/mo for cloud storage. Person/vehicle/animal classification.
- **Gianteye 4G LTE Solar Camera**: ~$60. 2K, no WiFi needed (uses SIM card), solar-battery. AI human detection. Built-in 64GB storage. Specifically mentions construction sites in marketing.
- **eufy SoloCam S220**: ~$42 (renewed). Solar, wireless. AI detection.
- **Ring Battery Cam**: $50-$100. AI person detection, customizable alerts, solar panel add-on.

### The gap
- Commercial AI security: $500-$5,000/month — designed for multi-million-dollar commercial projects
- Consumer AI cameras: $40-$100/unit, $0-$15/month — designed for homeowners watching their porches
- **Nobody packages consumer-grade AI cameras as a residential construction security solution**
- Residential builder building a $400K home doesn't budget $1,500/month for ECAM
- But three $80 Wyze cameras = $240 total + $15/mo cloud = $420/year
- Preventing ONE $2,500 theft pays for the system 6x over
- At 2% shrinkage on a $400K home, that's $8,000 in theft exposure vs. $420 in camera costs

### Original calculation
- Median new single-family home sale price: ~$420,000 (Census Bureau 2025)
- Industry shrinkage estimate: 2% of gross revenue = $8,400 per home
- AI camera system cost: 4 solar cameras @ $80 = $320 + $15/mo × 6 months = $410
- ROI: $8,400 ÷ $410 = 20:1 return if shrinkage eliminated
- Even at 25% shrinkage reduction: $2,100 saved vs. $410 spent = 5:1 return
- Insurance angle: typical builder's risk deductible $2,500-$5,000 — most thefts fall below, so insurance doesn't help
