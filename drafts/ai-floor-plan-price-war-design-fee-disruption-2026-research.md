# Research: AI Floor Plan Price War — Design Fee Disruption 2026

**Slug:** ai-floor-plan-price-war-design-fee-disruption-2026
**Journalist:** Elena Vasquez (architecture & design)
**Date:** September 10, 2026
**Kill test:** PASS. Anyone about to build a custom home or ADU needs to know what a $20/month AI plan generator actually replaces (a week of schematic sketching) and what it does not (the stamped permit set, the structural engineering, the copyright on the design). This decides whether the AI is a $60 shortcut or a $6,500 false economy. That is a person with a project on the line.

## Working headline
"Your AI Drew a Beautiful Floor Plan in Nine Minutes. It Still Costs $6,500 to Build From."

## Angle
AI floor-plan generators have crashed the price of *schematic* design to roughly $1.33 per layout (Maket: $20/month, 300 credits, 20 credits per generation). But residential design fees were never mostly schematic work: the money lives in construction documents, structural coordination, code compliance, and the licensed stamp. Original contribution: a fully-costed four-path comparison for a 2,000 sq ft home (AI-hybrid, stock plan, draftsperson/bid-set, full custom architect) showing that AI compresses the cheapest phase of design while the expensive phases cost the same regardless of who sketched the first plan. Plus a copyright hole nobody mentions: under Copyright Office guidance, a purely AI-generated plan cannot be registered, so the builder who paid $299 for an AI plan owns less than the builder who bought a $1,800 stock plan with a license.

## Primary sources

### 1. Maket.ai — official pricing and FAQ (maket.ai, primary company source)
- Homeowner plan: $20/month, 300 credits; each floor plan generation costs 20 credits (15 generations per month). Free tier: 50 one-time credits, no card required.
- Pro plan: $100/month, 3,000 credits, up to five human-validated plan uploads per month.
- Company states AI editing "handles roughly 70 to 75 percent of the schematic floor plan phase" and that "your architect still stamps the permit set."
- "They don't replace licensed professionals for structural engineering, mechanical systems, or local building code compliance."
- URLs: https://www.maket.ai/blog/how-to-use-maket ; https://www.maket.ai/blog/ai-editing-floor-plans ; https://www.maket.ai/blog/what-does-ai-home-design-software-actually-do

### 2. illustrarch.com — Maket AI review (industry press, Sep 2026)
- Credit pricing table: Free $0/50 credits; Homeowner $20/300; Pro $100/3,000; Scale custom.
- "Generated plans are a starting point, and the Pro tier's human-validated uploads exist precisely because the automated output is not a drawing you build from."
- Iteration costs credits and "generative work is iteration by definition, so the useful question is not the monthly rate but how many attempts a real project takes."
- URL: https://illustrarch.com/articles/design-softwares/73352-maket-ai-review.html

### 3. The House Plan Company — stock vs custom cost comparison (citing NAHB and HUD)
- 2,500 sq ft worked example: stock plan $1,800 + minor modifications $1,200 + engineering review $800 = $3,800 total (~1% of construction budget), 2-4 weeks to permit application.
- Custom: architectural design $10,000-$15,000 + engineering $2,000 + consultations $1,500 = $13,500-$18,500 (5-6% of construction), 3-6 months.
- NAHB: stock plan base $1,000-$3,000; modifications $500-$2,000; engineering $500-$1,200.
- HUD research: modified stock plans deliver ~70% of the customization at roughly 25-30% of the custom design cost.
- NAHB 2024 Construction Cost Survey: average new single-family home construction cost $428,215, ~$162/sq ft, average size 2,647 sq ft.
- URL: https://www.thehouseplancompany.com/blog/cost-comparison-stock-vs-custom-house-plans-what-homeowners-need-to-know/

### 4. Coohom — architect fee breakdown (citing NAHB)
- Three fee models: percentage of construction cost 5%-15%; per square foot $2-$10 (plans) to $15+ (full service); flat fee $3,000-$8,000 for simple 1,200-2,000 sq ft plans, $30,000+ for complex custom.
- Nashville homeowner case: DIY digital layout draft + architect hourly consult and permit plans = $5,600 total, "half what her neighbor paid for a similar house using full-service design from day one."
- URL: https://www.coohom.com/article/how-much-do-architects-cost-for-house-plans-get-real-insights

### 5. aitools.fyi — Floor Plan AI pricing (directory, Sep 2026 data)
- Starter plan $9.99/month includes 200 credits; Basic AI generation costs 1 credit per layout (~$0.05 per generated layout); Pro mode 4 credits per generation.
- Exports DXF, PDF, PNG, JPG; browser-based, no CAD install.
- URL: https://aitools.fyi/floor-plan-ai

### 6. U.S. Copyright Office — Registration Guidance, 88 Fed. Reg. 16190 (March 16, 2023)
- "Copyright protection in the United States requires human authorship. If a work's traditional elements of authorship were produced by a machine, the work lacks human authorship and the Office will not register it."
- Applicants must disclose AI-generated material beyond de minimis and describe the human author's contribution.
- Architectural plans are copyrightable subject matter when human-authored (Architectural Works Copyright Protection Act, 17 U.S.C. 101/102(a)(8)); purely AI-generated plans fall outside it.
- URL: https://copyright.gov/laws/hearings/USCO-Letter-on-AI-and-Copyright-Initiative-Update-Feb-23-2024.pdf (summarizing the 2023 guidance)

## Original contribution: the four-path permit math (2,000 sq ft home)
Assumptions: construction cost $162/sq ft (NAHB 2024 survey) x 2,000 = $324,000 construction. Schematic phase is roughly 15% of a full architectural fee (industry standard phase breakdown: schematic design ~15%, design development ~20%, construction documents ~40%, bidding ~5%, construction administration ~20%).

| Path | Schematic cost | Permit-ready cost | Total design cost | % of construction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI-hybrid (Maket 3 mo + architect consult/stamp + engineering) | $60 (3 x $20/mo) | ~$5,600 stamp/consult + $1,000 engineering | ~$6,660 | ~2.1% |
| Stock plan + mods + engineering | included in plan | $1,800 plan + $1,200 mods + $800 eng | ~$3,800 | ~1.2% |
| Draftsperson permit set + engineering | n/a | $2,500-$4,000 + $1,000 eng | ~$3,500-$5,000 | ~1.1-1.5% |
| Full custom architect (8% fee) | $3,888 (15% of fee) | $22,032 remainder | ~$25,920 | 8.0% |

The finding: AI compresses the schematic line from ~$3,900 to $60, a 98% cut, but schematic work was only 15% of the fee. The remaining 85% (construction documents, code compliance, structural coordination, stamp) costs the same whoever sketched the first plan. Net savings vs. full custom: ~74%, real but concentrated entirely in the front of the process. And vs. the stock-plan path, the AI-hybrid path is actually $2,800 MORE expensive because stock plans ship with construction documents included while AI plans ship with none.

## Limitations
- Maket's "70-75% of the schematic phase" is a vendor claim, not independently verified; actual time savings depend on user skill and iteration count (each iteration burns credits).
- The Nashville $5,600 hybrid case is a single anecdote from a Coohom article; local architect consult/stamp fees vary widely ($1,500-$5,000+ by market).
- Stock plan engineering review ($500-$1,200) assumes a simple lot; seismic/wind/snow zones add structural engineering costs to every path equally.
- The copyright point is an inference from the 2023 guidance applied to architectural plans; no court has ruled specifically on AI-generated residential floor plans. An AI-assisted plan with substantial human modification likely clears the human-authorship bar.
- Credit pricing changes frequently; Maket's per-generation cost was 20 credits at time of research.

## Strongest counterargument
The stock-plan comparison is unfair to AI: stock plans are someone else's house, and AI generates YOUR house, which is the entire point of custom design. If the alternative is a $26,000 architect fee, the AI-hybrid path at ~$6,700 is a genuine 74% saving that makes custom design accessible to people who would otherwise buy a stock plan or not build at all. The democratization argument is real, and architects' guild-style fee percentages have been ripe for disruption since long before AI.
