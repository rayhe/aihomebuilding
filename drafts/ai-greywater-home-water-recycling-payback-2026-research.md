# Research: ai-greywater-home-water-recycling-payback-2026
**Article #753 — Priya Greenwood (Sustainability & Green Building)**
**Ship after:** 2026-09-11 (queue: #745→09-03 … #752→09-10)
**Date:** September 2, 2026

## Angle
Two ways to reuse your home's graywater: a $150 laundry-to-landscape weekend project (often net-free after utility rebates, no permit needed in CA) versus a $4,000+ smart treatment box (Hydraloop) whose electricity bill nearly eats its water savings. Original contribution: head-to-head payback math including the treatment unit's power draw, which nobody in the trade press has published.

## Kill test
Helps a homeowner decide: should I replumb for graywater, buy the smart box, or do the cheap hose? Yes — concrete dollar math and a decision rule.

## Original math (methodology)
Assumptions stated in article: 4-person household; CA urban blended water rate $8/kgal (EPA national water-only avg is $6.06; CA combined water+sewer commonly $10–15/kgal, so $8 water-only is conservative); CA residential electricity ~$0.32/kWh.

**Laundry-to-landscape:** LADWP formula: 15 gal/day/person × 7 × 4 = 420 gal/week = 21,840 gal/yr diverted to landscape. × $8/kgal = **~$175/yr** avoided water cost. Parts $100–200 (3-way diverter, tubing, mulch basins); Valley Water rebate $200–400 → net cost ≤ $0 in service area. Payback: under 1 year, often day one.

**Hydraloop:** vendor's own 4,676 gal/person/yr × 4 = 18,704 gal/yr × $8/kgal = **~$150/yr** water savings. Power draw 350 kWh/yr × $0.32 = **~$112/yr** electricity. Net ≈ **$38/yr** → payback on $4,000 unit alone: 100+ years; with $2,000–5,000 dual-plumbing retrofit: never in the unit's service life. At punishing $14/kgal tiered rates: $262 − $112 = $150/yr → 27-yr payback on unit alone, still poor.

## Limitations
- $8/kgal is a modeling assumption; actual rates vary 3× across CA utilities. Readers should check their bill.
- 350 kWh/yr comes from a 2021 secondary writeup of the H600, not a metered independent test; newer models may differ.
- Hydraloop savings figure (17,703 L/person/yr) is vendor-published, EU-context, no third-party audit.
- Sewer-bill savings from graywater diversion not modeled (many CA utilities bill sewer on winter water use; diversion may or may not reduce it).
- No lifecycle analysis of the treatment unit's embodied carbon vs. saved water-treatment energy.

## Strongest counterargument
Dollars aren't the only currency: in a drought with mandatory cutbacks, 18,000 gal/yr of drought-proof irrigation water keeps $30,000 of landscaping alive when the district bans outdoor watering. For new construction, dual plumbing roughed in at framing costs a few hundred dollars, which transforms the retrofit math. And at commercial scale (the Les Echos piece), the H600's 5–10 year payback is real. The box isn't a scam; it's a new-construction and commercial product being sold to retrofit homeowners.

## Decision rule (actionable)
- Existing CA home with washer near an exterior wall → L2L this weekend; check Valley Water/EBMUD/Petaluma rebates first; no permit needed; switch to boron-free detergent.
- Building new → rough in dual plumbing now ($300–600 at framing), decide on treatment later.
- Buy the $4,000 box only if: new construction with dual plumbing already, or water over ~$14/kgal, or you value drought-proofing above payback.
