# Research Notes: Crawl Space Monitoring AI Gap

## Core Angle
27 million American homes have crawl spaces. IoT sensors + AI algorithms exist to continuously monitor moisture, temperature, and structural movement in these inaccessible spaces. Commercial buildings use them. Nobody sells an integrated system to homeowners. The gap between what's technically possible and what's commercially available is enormous.

## Key Data Points

### Prevalence
- ~27 million US homes have crawl spaces (15% of housing stock) — HomeLight/Census
- Only 9.2% of NEW homes built on crawl spaces in 2024 (down from higher historically) — NAHB/Census Survey of Construction 2024
- 73% of new homes on slabs, 17% basements — NAHB Eye on Housing 2024
- Crawl space usage declining but massive installed base remains

### The Confidence Gap (Groundworks/Atomik Research, June 2026)
- Foundation repair demand surged 20%+ in first 5 months of 2026
- 86% of homeowners believe home is structurally sound
- 82% feel in full control of long-term safety
- 36% admit they lack knowledge/confidence to check for foundation/basement problems
- Survey: n=2,000 homeowners, ±2pp at 95% CI, fieldwork May 27-Jun 2, 2026
- Nearly half of all US homes built before 1980 (First American Data, ~161M homes)
- 38.4 million homes built 1970-1990 entering 40-year structural service window
- South: 39% uncovered structural problem worse/more costly than expected (highest of any region)

### Repair Costs
- Crawl space encapsulation: $5,000-$15,000 average (Angi 2026 data)
- Moisture/water damage repair: $1,000-$9,800
- Structural repairs: $1,500-$15,000
- Mold remediation: $500-$4,000
- Sub-floor rot: $4,800-$7,200
- Foundation waterproofing: $1,500-$15,000 (avg $4,600)
- Labor: 50-70% of encapsulation costs

### What Exists (Commercial/Niche)
- OmniSense S-11 sensors: ~$50 each, gateway $200-$450 (Fine Homebuilding)
  - Monitor temp, RH, absolute humidity, wood moisture content
  - Wireless, battery-powered (5-20 year life)
  - Cloud-based streaming data
  - Used by building science professionals, not consumers
- VILPE Sense (Finland): crawl space humidity control system
  - IoT sensors + algorithm comparing indoor/outdoor absolute humidity
  - Controls EC roof fan ventilation on demand
  - New and existing buildings
  - Not consumer-marketed in US
- Sensor Innovation into® Control (Norway): AI-powered building moisture monitoring
  - Real-time, long-term monitoring
  - Hybrid-AI: ML algorithms + building physics
  - SMS/email/BMS alerts
  - Commercial/institutional focus
- Hosta a.i. (MIT spinoff): photo-based property condition assessment
  - Can detect material conditions, damage, risks from photos
  - Insurance/contractor/lender focused
- Ring Alarm Flood & Freeze Sensor: $35 each (needs $200 base station)
  - Detects water contact and temperature
  - No crawl space specific features
  - No predictive analytics

### The Gap
- Commercial buildings: extensive monitoring (BMS, IoT, predictive maintenance)
- Residential crawl spaces: essentially no continuous monitoring
- Most homeowners enter crawl space 0-1 times/year
- Home inspectors spend 10-15 minutes in crawl space during purchase inspection
- No integrated consumer product combines: moisture sensors, temperature, structural tilt/movement, pest detection, radon, AI prediction
