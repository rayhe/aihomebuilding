# Research: Underground Utility Mapping — Residential Builder Gap

## Core Thesis
Underground utility strikes hit a record high in 2025 (221,717 reported damages, CGA DIRT 2025). The total cost to the US economy: $30 billion annually. The 3D mapping technology that prevents these strikes exists — GPR, electromagnetic locators, AR visualization, centimeter-accurate 3D models — but it's built for commercial infrastructure projects, not residential builders. A homebuilder digging a foundation trench or running a sewer lateral relies on painted lines from an 811 call and hope. Even when they call, 25% of damages trace back to inaccurate maps and marking failures.

## Key Data Points

### Scale of the Problem
- **221,717 unique damage reports** in 2025 — record high (CGA DIRT 2025 Data Summary)
- **$30 billion annual cost** to US economy (CGA/DPAC estimate, confirmed by Roads & Bridges, Esri Community)
- Utility strikes happen **every 3 minutes** during construction (DPAC)
- CGA Index: rose to 102 in 2025 from baseline 100 in 2022, up 8 points from 2023
- **196,977 unique reported damages** in 2024 (CGA DIRT 2024, cited by Esri/NULCA)
- UK equivalent: 60,000 accidental strikes/year, £2.4bn cost (Utility Strike Avoidance Group)

### Root Causes (CGA DIRT 2024-2025)
- **Top 10 root causes = 86% of all damages**
- #1: Failure to notify 811 before excavation (leading cause, unchanged for years)
- **25% of damages** = locating and mapping accuracy failures
- **24% of damages** = excavation practices
- NULCA 2026 open letter: "Locating professionals have repeatedly described marks placed exactly where records indicated, only for excavation to reveal infrastructure in a completely different location. Not a few feet off. On the wrong side of the road. Crossing at the wrong block. Or not shown in the records at all."

### Damage by Utility Type (CGA 2025)
- Telecommunications/cable: 51%
- Natural gas: 36%
- Electric: 8%
- Water/sewer: 5%

### Construction Delay Impact
- **90% of all construction projects are delayed** (industry statistic, cited by Fugro)
- **50%+ of those delays are related to subsurface uncertainties** (Alexander van Noort, Fugro)

### Technology That Exists
- **Ground-penetrating radar (GPR):** Screening Eagle GS1000, real-time subsurface visualization
- **3D/AR visualization:** Trimble XR10 headsets overlaying subsurface data on job sites
- **SubVysion (YC S2026):** Centimeter-accurate 3D maps of underground utilities. "$30B in damages and 20 deaths every year in the US"
- **TrueWaveGPR (PBU UK):** Cuts survey time by 50%, works on challenging terrain
- **Hexagon:** Electromagnetic locators + GPS for utility detection and digital mapping
- **Underground utility mapping market:** Projected to reach $3.3B by 2030 (ResearchAndMarkets)
- **ASCE 38 standard:** Defines quality levels for utility data (Quality Level A through D)

### What Residential Builders Actually Use
- 811 call → painted marks on ground (approximate location, ±18-24 inches per industry standard)
- As-built drawings from municipality (often decades old, incomplete)
- Hand digging / soft digging near marks
- No GPR, no 3D models, no AR visualization, no digital record

### The Residential Gap
- All the mapping tech above is priced and marketed for commercial/infrastructure
- A residential GC running $300K-$2M projects isn't paying $5,000+ for a SUE survey
- But residential has unique risks: shallow foundations, tight lot lines, old neighborhoods with unmapped utilities
- Infill development and ADU construction in established neighborhoods = highest risk (old utilities, no records)
- Homeowner-initiated excavation (landscaping, fencing, pools) = no professional locating at all

### Cost of a Single Residential Strike
- Gas line repair: $3,000-$10,000 (contractor liability)
- Water main repair: $2,000-$8,000
- Fiber optic cable: $10,000-$50,000+ (business interruption claims)
- Project delay: 1-4 weeks average
- Insurance claim: deductible + premium increase
- In extreme cases: explosion, fire, injury, death (20 deaths/year nationally)

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? **YES.** If you're a builder breaking ground, you need to know what's under the dirt. If you're a homeowner adding an ADU or pool, you need to know too. The tech exists to tell you with centimeter accuracy. Nobody sells it to you.

## Angle
The 811 system works 99.47% of the time on the calls it gets. But the maps the locators use are wrong often enough to cause 221,717 strikes a year. The tech to fix this — centimeter-accurate 3D mapping — exists and is getting cheaper. But it's built for highway projects and data centers, not the GC pouring your foundation.
