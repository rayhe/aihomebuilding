# Research: AI Construction Waste Sorting — The Residential Scale Gap

## Thesis
AI-powered waste sorting and monitoring technology works at commercial construction scale (data centers, infrastructure, production builders) but fails economically at residential scale. A typical 2,000 sqft home generates 4 tons of waste — 60-80% of it divertible — yet most homes use a single mixed dumpster because the cost of AI monitoring and multi-bin sorting exceeds the savings. The mandates are coming (CALGreen 50% diversion, SB 1383 60% diversion), the technology exists, but the unit economics don't work for the ~80% of builders doing <25 homes/year.

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? YES — builders face tightening C&D waste diversion mandates (especially in CA) and need to understand whether AI waste monitoring is cost-effective at their scale. Homeowners pay for waste disposal ($1,800-$3,600+ per build in dumpster costs). This article helps builders and homeowners understand why waste diversion hasn't improved despite technology existing.

## Original Analysis

### Cost per home calculation:
- Typical 2,000 sqft home, 6-month build
- Waste generated: ~8,000 lbs (4 tons), ~50 cubic yards
- Dumpster needs: ~2-3 pulls of a 20-yard dumpster OR 1-2 pulls of a 30-yard
- Cost estimate:
  - Dumpster rental: 2-3 pulls × $450 = $900-$1,350
  - Tipping/disposal (at $103/ton for C&D): 4 tons × $103 = $412
  - Haul fees: 2-3 × $249 = $498-$747
  - TOTAL: ~$1,810 - $2,509 per home
  - Higher-cost markets (CA, NY, WA): $3,000-$5,000+

### AI monitoring cost-benefit at residential scale:
- Camera + software: ~$200-500/month per dumpster site
- Multi-bin setup (wood/drywall/cardboard/mixed): 4 containers instead of 1
  - Additional space: ~600 sqft vs ~100 sqft for single dumpster
  - Additional haul costs: 4× instead of 1×
  - Sorted material disposal: lower tipping (recycled wood ~$30/ton, drywall ~$40/ton vs $103/ton mixed)
- Savings from sorting: ~$150-250/home (reduced tipping on sorted materials)
- AI monitoring cost spread over 1 home: $1,200-3,000 (6-month build)
- AI monitoring cost spread over 40 concurrent homes: $30-75/home
- BREAKEVEN: ~40+ concurrent active dumpsters

### The scale threshold:
- 80% of US homebuilders build <25 homes/year (NAHB Census data)
- Even a builder doing 25 homes/year only has ~10-15 active sites at any time
- The 40-dumpster breakeven requires a production builder doing 100-200+ homes/year
- That's the top 5-10% of builders by volume
