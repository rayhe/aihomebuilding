# Research: AI Wildfire Risk Scoring — The Homeowner Transparency Gap

## Angle
AI-powered wildfire risk models can now score individual homes down to the property level, predicting destruction risk days before a fire arrives. Commercial underwriters have dashboards with forward-looking intelligence. Homeowners get a non-renewal letter and no explanation. The AI that scores your home won't tell you the score.

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? **YES.** If you're building in a wildfire-prone area, your home's AI wildfire risk score determines whether you can get insurance and what you'll pay. You can't mitigate what you can't measure.

## Primary Sources

### 1. ZestyAI / Morningstar Analysis (Jan 2026)
- **1.2 million California properties** rated high wildfire risk by AI models but low/no risk on FEMA maps
- **$940 billion** in property value in these "blind spots"
- Only **37.5%** of California properties carry any FEMA wildfire rating; **62.5% have no federal assessment at all**
- **300,859** blind-spot homes were built before 1980, predating fire-resistant building codes
- Among high-hazard properties (score ≥7): **41.3% reduced home-destruction risk through mitigation** — but this may not be reflected in their insurance
- Among extreme-hazard properties (score ≥9): **42.5% improved** via mitigation, but **52% saw regional hazard worsen** (avg +1.3 points = **51% jump in annual wildfire probability**)
- Source: https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260108to59386/nearly-1-trillion-in-california-homes-labeled-low-risk-despite-elevated-wildfire-danger

### 2. Washington State SB 5928 — Wildfire Risk Score Transparency (Feb 2026)
- Passed state Senate **48-1** (bipartisan, only 1 dissent)
- Sponsored by Insurance Commissioner Patty Kuderer, prime sponsor Sen. Judy Warnick (R-Moses Lake)
- Requires insurers to: disclose wildfire risk scores, explain factors, provide steps to improve score
- Creates **appeal right** for homeowners who've done mitigation or see inaccuracies
- Supported by: WA Hospitality Assoc, WA Realtors, Confederated Tribes of Colville Reservation, WA Fire Chiefs
- Source: https://www.insurance.wa.gov/about-us/news/2026/wildfire-risk-scoring-transparency-bill-passes-state-senate

### 3. California's FAIR Plan Explosion
- FAIR Plan dwelling policies grew **123%** in 4 years: 202,897 (Sept 2020) → 451,799 (Sept 2024)
- Exposure: $153B (2020) → $458B (2024)
- Written premium: $424M (FY2021) → $1.267B (FY2024) — **199% increase**
- Would rank as **3rd-largest market share** in CA by premium
- **642,010** active FAIR Plan policies (Sept 2025); **90% (580,000)** in high/very high wildfire risk counties
- Contra Costa County: **1,073% FAIR Plan growth** (1,094 → 12,837 policies, 2021-2025)
- 46 of 58 California counties seeing private insurer exit
- Source: Milliman analysis (https://www.milliman.com/en/insight/california-homeowners-insurance-los-angeles-wildfires), MoneyGeek analysis

### 4. California's Mitigation Loophole
- CDI regulation preserves loophole: insurers can ignore mitigation efforts for non-renewal decisions
- Consumer Watchdog called out loophole 3 times in public comments; Commissioner Lara didn't close it
- Former Insurance Commissioner Steve Poizner's own home non-renewed despite "tens of thousands of dollars" in required mitigation
- Prop 103 gives commissioner authority to require mitigation discounts and disclosure — not exercised
- Source: Consumer Watchdog (https://consumerwatchdog.org), CDI regulatory filing

### 5. Property Guardian's Forward-Looking Intelligence
- Wildfire Recurrence Risk Score: incorporates time-aware fire history + fuel dynamics
- **Predicted LA wildfire destruction areas 2-3 days before fire arrival** (Jan 2025)
- Partnership with EigenRisk and Convr — commercial underwriters get this data
- Homeowners do not
- Source: BusinessWire/Property Guardian (June 2026)

### 6. Real Human Cost
- Canyon Park Villas (San Diego): insurance went from **$47,000/year to $600,000/year** (13x increase)
- 240 units, many seniors on fixed income, $2,500 immediate special assessment per home
- Standard Guaranty: 100% non-renewal in Lake Arrowhead, Twin Peaks, Wrightwood, Idyllwild, Chatsworth, Encinitas
- Verisk FireLine scores range 0-30, but homeowners rarely see their actual score
- Source: Reuters, Insurify

## Novel Analysis
Cross-reference ZestyAI's finding that 41.3% of high-risk homeowners invested in mitigation with the fact that California's regulation lets insurers ignore that mitigation for non-renewal decisions. The AI model sees the improvement. The insurer's non-renewal process doesn't have to account for it.

## Strongest Counterargument
Insurers argue that property-level scoring is more accurate than the old ZIP-code-level assessments — and they're right. The shift from geographic averaging to AI property-level scoring means lower-risk homes in high-risk areas can potentially keep coverage (if the insurer uses the data that way). The problem is that transparency and appeal rights haven't kept pace with the scoring technology.
