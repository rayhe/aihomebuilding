# Research: Construction Misclassification and AI Payroll Fraud Detection Gap

## Angle
Up to 2.1 million construction workers are illegally misclassified or paid off the books. AI payroll compliance tools exist that can detect fraud through GPS tracking, BLE sensors, and predictive analytics — but they're built for commercial general contractors. The residential sector, where misclassification is most concentrated, operates on cash, informal labor brokers, and zero digital infrastructure.

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? YES — misclassification means your residential contractor's workers have no workers' comp, no safety training, no OSHA protections. If a worker falls off your roof and has no coverage, you could face liability. It also means your low bid was subsidized by illegal labor practices.

## The Gap
AI payroll compliance tools require:
1. Digital enrollment of every worker (GPS smartphone, BLE badge)
2. Connected infrastructure (cameras, sensors, dashboards)
3. Employer participation in formal workers' comp programs
4. $5-15/worker/month subscription costs

Residential construction operates with:
1. Cash payments, no digital records
2. Informal labor brokers recruiting day laborers
3. Many employers not registered with state UI systems
4. Workers who fear reporting due to immigration status
5. Subcontracting chains 3-5 layers deep

Result: The AI tools that detect fraud, protect workers, and reduce comp claims only work for employers who are already following the law. The ones committing fraud operate outside any digital system the AI can see.
