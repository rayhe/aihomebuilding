# Research: AI Remodel ROI — Cost vs Value Meets the AI Estimator
Slug: `ai-remodel-roi-cost-vs-value-estimator-2026`
Journalist: Frank "The Foreman" DeLuca (Project Management & Operations)
Date: 2026-10-05

## Kill test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? **Yes.** Anyone pricing a remodel before listing, or deciding between projects on a fixed budget, gets a decision rule: which projects return money, which burn it, and when an AI cost estimate is trustworthy enough to act on.

## Angle
Zonda's 2025 Cost vs. Value report says a $4,672 garage door returns $12,526 and a $161,511 upscale kitchen returns $57,380. AI estimators (Kukun, Plunk, Realtor.com's Kukun-powered calculator) now hand homeowners instant cost estimates, claiming ~80% accuracy. Original contribution: the estimator's own stated error band (±20%) is wider than the profit margin on the only profitable interior project — so the AI is decision-grade exactly where the decision is already obvious, and decoration where it matters.

## Original contribution: the error-band calculation
Kukun's stated accuracy target (80%) implies roughly a ±20% band around any AI cost estimate. Apply it to the CvV numbers:

- **Minor kitchen** (cost $28,458, value $32,141, margin +$3,683): estimate 20% high → implied cost $34,150 → ROI 94% (below break-even). Estimate 20% low → $22,766 → ROI 141%. The error band (±$5,692) is wider than the entire profit margin. The AI's own uncertainty decides the verdict on the only profitable interior project.
- **Garage door** (cost $4,672, value $12,526, margin +$7,854): estimate 20% high → $5,606 → ROI still 224%. Conclusion unchanged.
- **Upscale kitchen** (cost $161,511, value $57,380): even a 20%-low estimate ($129,209) → ROI 44%. Conclusion unchanged: it destroys money.
- Regional swing: Pacific minor kitchen nets +$8,656 ($29,728/$38,384); West North Central nets −$1,547 ($27,880/$26,333). Same scope, $10,203 swing by region. A national-average AI estimate cannot see this.
- Net-dollar framing: minor kitchen +$3,683 nationally; upscale kitchen −$104,131; upscale primary suite −$274,556 ($338,323 − $63,767); garage door +$7,854; steel door +$2,835 ($5,270 − $2,435).

## Strongest counterargument (to state at full strength)
ROI is the wrong metric for most remodelers. Only 18% remodel to sell (NAR). The Joy Scores say the kitchen and primary suite — the two worst financial investments — deliver the most happiness. A family that cooks dinner together for ten years in an upscale kitchen got its money's worth in a currency the CvV report doesn't measure. The Redfin study's 69% average and Harvard's "art not science" warn that even the best dataset is agent opinion layered on noisy transactions. And AI estimators are lead-generation funnels: Kukun's endgame is matching you with contractors (the "Kukun score"), so the estimate is the top of a sales funnel, not a fiduciary opinion.

## Limitations to state
- CvV resale values are real-estate professionals' estimates of value added, not observed transaction premiums; methodology is survey-based.
- Did not independently test Kukun, Plunk, or Realtor.com's calculator; accuracy claims are vendor statements.
- Zonda's data-licensing terms limit reproduction; figures cited narratively with attribution.
- Regional figures used (Pacific, West North Central, Spokane example at 81%/$21,198) show national averages mislead; reader's market will differ.
- Cost data reflects pre-tariff baselines; Zonda's 2025 downgrade analysis suggests realized scopes are shrinking.
- Not financial advice; verify with local bids and a licensed appraiser.

## Actionable takeaways (for the article)
1. If selling within 2 years: work the CvV table from the top down — garage door, steel entry door, stone veneer — before touching interiors.
2. If staying 5+ years: optimize for Joy Score, not ROI; the math flips.
3. Treat AI estimates as ±20%: only trust the verdict when the project clears the band (garage door yes; minor kitchen — get real bids).
4. Check your region: Pacific vs. West North Central swings a minor kitchen from +$8,656 to −$1,547 net.
5. Minor scope beats major scope on every interior project: the $28K kitchen nets +$3,683; the $161K kitchen nets −$104K.
