# Research: AI Construction Site Security — The Residential Gap

## Thesis
AI-powered jobsite cameras can detect intruders, track equipment, and trigger real-time alerts on commercial construction sites. But residential builders — who collectively lose hundreds of millions to theft annually — are stuck between a $150 consumer camera with no construction intelligence and a $600/month enterprise system designed for $50M projects. The tools exist. The economics don't fit.

## Key Data Points

### Theft Scale
- **$300M–$1B**: Annual US construction theft losses (NER/NICB estimates, equipment alone)
- **11,000+**: Theft incidents reported to FBI annually — surpasses convenience store thefts
- **70%**: Workers who witness theft on job sites annually (BauWatch 2024 Construction Crime Index)
- **<25%**: Recovery rate for stolen construction equipment (some studies put it at 7%)
- **One-third** of all projects experience delays from criminal activity

### Per-Incident Costs (MobileVideoGuard)
| Item Stolen | Typical Cost Per Incident |
|---|---|
| Heavy equipment (excavator, bulldozer) | $50,000 – $500,000 |
| Copper wiring and piping | $5,000 – $10,000 |
| Power tools and generators | $5,000 – $30,000 |
| Lumber and building materials | $2,000 – $15,000 |
| HVAC equipment (installed) | $10,000 – $40,000 |
| Fuel theft | $500 – $5,000 |

### Hidden Costs
- 2–3 day work stoppage costs $10,000–$50,000 (crew standby, equipment rental, penalties)
- Insurance premiums rise 15–30% after 2+ theft claims
- OSHA estimates unplanned stoppages cost $12,000/day on mid-size projects
- Legal/investigation costs: $1,000–$5,000 per incident (rarely reimbursed)

### AI Camera Solutions (Commercial-Focused)
- **TrueLook**: $99–$600/month software; hardware separate; AI motion detection, PPE monitoring, 4K
- **OxBlue**: "Higher-end pricing"; 10-year warranty; AI dashboards; government/commercial focus
- **Sensera**: Solar-powered, LTE; commercial jobsite focus
- **EarthCam**: Ultra-high-res, GigapixelCam; megaproject focus; $$$
- **Spot AI**: Enterprise portfolios, data security focus
- Total cost for managed system: $200–$600/month per camera + hardware

### Consumer Alternatives
- **Reolink Argus B440**: $119.99, 4K, solar+battery, WiFi, basic PIR motion
- **Ring Spotlight Cam Plus**: $149.99, 1080p, battery, WiFi, motion zones
- **Tactacam DEFEND**: $119.99–$199.99, cellular, battery, PIR
- No time-lapse, no AI-powered detection, no construction-specific analytics
- No Procore/Autodesk integration
- Won't distinguish a subcontractor from a trespasser

### The Math Problem
- Average single-family home build: ~$400K (2024 Census)
- Typical build timeline: 6–9 months
- TrueLook Premium (AI + 24/7 recording): $400–$600/month × 7 months = $2,800–$4,200 + hardware
- Single copper theft event: $5,000–$10,000
- Break-even: one prevented theft pays for the system
- But: most residential builders operate on 5–10% margins, making any "nice to have" expenditure hard to justify
- Most residential theft happens on spec homes and weekend/evening hours

### ServiceTitan 2026 Report
- 74% of residential contractors see AI as efficiency key
- Only 25% currently using AI
- Gap between interest and adoption is massive

### UK Comparison (BauWatch)
- £1B+ annual theft losses (2025)
- 92% of construction companies experienced theft
- 55% of sites experience theft at least twice per year
- 67% witnessed increase in site crime (nearly double European average)

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? **YES.** If you're building a home, your builder's insurance deductible is probably higher than the cost of a consumer camera. But consumer cameras can't tell the difference between your framing crew arriving at 6 AM and someone cutting your copper at midnight. This article tells you what works, what doesn't, and what the actual ROI looks like.
