# Research: AI Scheduling — Custom Home vs. Production Builder Gap

## Core Thesis
AI scheduling tools can simulate 50+ ways to build a project and cut timelines by 17%. But they require BIM models and Primavera P6 — tools custom homebuilders have never touched. Custom homes take 12.8 months vs. 7.4 months for spec/production homes. The scheduling sophistication gap is the largest single factor, and nobody's building the tool that closes it for residential.

## Primary Sources

### 1. Census Bureau / NAHB: Construction Timelines
- **Custom homes:** 12.8 months from permit to completion (2021 Survey of Construction)
- **Built-for-sale (production):** 7.4 months from permit to completion
- **Contractor-built on owner's land:** 10.5 months
- **Built-for-rent:** 11.1 months
- **Average single-family:** 8.6 months in 2023 (peaked), declining in 2024-2025
- **Regional variation:** New England longest (11.9 mo), Mountain shortest (8.3 mo)
- Source: NAHB analysis of Census Bureau 2021 Survey of Construction (Pro Builder, Builder Online)

### 2. AI Scheduling Adoption Rates
- **Only 16% of contractors** apply AI/automation to scheduling (Q2 2026)
- **60% of contractors** report NO plans to adopt AI scheduling
- "One of the widest opportunity gaps in the current data"
- Source: Construction Week Online, Q2 2026 data

### 3. Residential AI Adoption
- **25% of residential contractors** use AI meaningfully (ServiceTitan 2026 Residential State of the Trades, 1,000+ contractors surveyed)
- **49% of single-family homebuilders** use AI in some capacity (NAHB/Wells Fargo HMI, Aug 2025)
- **Of AI users:** 20% for advertising, 11% for analysis/planning, <0.5% for safety
- **Nearly half lack trust in AI** (ServiceTitan)
- **92% of contractors** have no formal AI training (Houzz survey, May 2026)
- Source: HousingWire (ServiceTitan report), Homes.com (NAHB/Wells Fargo), Bisnow (Houzz)

### 4. Commercial AI Adoption
- AI adoption jumped from 17% to 38% in one year among commercial contractors (ServiceTitan 2026)
- 24% using AI for cost estimation, 22% for bid management
- 78% of contractors using or testing AI (BuildOps survey, 600+ U.S./Canada)
- Source: LinkedIn/GlobeNewsWire (ServiceTitan), LinkedIn (BuildOps)

### 5. ALICE Technologies (Leading AI Scheduler)
- **17% reduction in project duration**, 14% labor cost reduction, 12% equipment cost reduction
- Customers: Zachry Construction, Suffolk Construction ($8B revenue, 3,000+ employees), Andrade Gutierrez, Implenia
- Requires: BIM models + Oracle Primavera P6 schedules
- McKinsey partnership for "large capital projects"
- "Imagine you can build your product 50 times before you build it" — CEO René Morkos
- Zachry reduced highway project timeline by 28 days
- Pricing: declined to share
- Targets: infrastructure, industrial, commercial. NOT residential.
- Source: ENR, Construction Dive, Bisnow

### 6. Why Residential Is Stuck
- **#1 barrier isn't cost — it's disconnected systems**: Only 20% of contractors on single platform (ServiceTitan). Rest bounce between accounting, PM, estimating tools that don't talk.
- **Harvard JCHS:** Over half of residential remodeling businesses generate <$250K/year revenue — too small for enterprise AI tools
- **No BIM:** Residential builders don't use BIM (which AI schedulers require)
- **No P6:** Residential builders use Excel, MS Project, or paper — not Oracle Primavera
- **Culture:** "They're experimenting but don't have it embedded in workflows" — Angie Snow, ServiceTitan
- **NAHB response:** Published first 246-page AI resource for builders (BuilderBooks)

### 7. Market Size
- AI in construction market: ~$13B (2026) → ~$28B (2031) 
- Source: HousingWire

### 8. The Custom Home Scheduling Problem
- Custom builds have unique architectural features, site-specific conditions, one-off material orders
- Production builders repeat the same plan dozens of times — they've optimized their scheduling through iteration
- Custom builders coordinate 15-25 subcontractor trades with no standardized sequence
- No feedback loop: each custom home is a one-off, so scheduling lessons don't transfer
- Trade coordination is manual: phone calls, texts, personal relationships

## Angle
Frank DeLuca — operations/project management. The 5.4-month gap between custom and production builds is a scheduling problem. The AI that closes it in commercial (ALICE, nPlan) doesn't work for residential because it needs BIM and P6. Nobody's building the residential-grade version. The custom homebuilder with 10 homes/year is stuck with Excel and gut instinct.

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? **YES.**
- If you're building custom: ask your builder what scheduling tool they use. If the answer is "Excel" or "I keep it in my head," that's a 5-month tax.
- If you're buying new construction: production builders are faster because their scheduling is optimized, not because the work is simpler.
- ROI: A 17% reduction on a 12.8-month custom build = 2.2 months saved. At carrying costs of $3,000-5,000/month (land loan interest, rent while waiting), that's $6,600-$11,000 in savings.
