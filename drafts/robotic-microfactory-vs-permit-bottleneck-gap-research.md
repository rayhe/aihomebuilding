# Research: The Robot Builds Faster Than the City Can Permit

## Angle
The Cosmic/ABB mobile microfactory can fabricate a house in 12 weeks. Los Angeles County takes 95 business days on average to issue a new residential construction permit. The most advanced construction robot on earth is bottlenecked by paperwork. One year after 16,000+ structures burned, seven homes have been completed.

This is about the gap between construction automation and permitting automation. The industry is pouring billions into building houses faster while the regulatory pipeline that controls whether you can start building operates at 1970s speed.

## Kill Test
Does this help someone building or buying a home? YES. If you're rebuilding after a disaster, or building in a jurisdiction with permit backlogs, the construction speed of your builder is irrelevant if the permit takes 5x longer than the build.

## Original Analysis

### The Timeline Math
For a typical fire victim in Pacific Palisades:
1. Debris removal: 3-6 months (mostly completed by mid-2025 for phase 1)
2. Insurance claim resolution: ongoing, many unresolved
3. Permit application + review: 95 business days average = ~19 weeks / ~4.5 months
4. Construction (traditional): 12-18 months for custom home
5. Construction (Cosmic microfactory): 12 weeks / ~3 months

Total traditional: ~22-30 months
Total with Cosmic: ~10-13 months (but still bottlenecked at step 3)

Construction is only ~50-60% of the timeline traditionally, and only ~23-30% with Cosmic.
Even if construction time is reduced to zero, the permit bottleneck alone means no home is completed in under 4.5 months.

### Scale Gap
- Cosmic: 35 families served, goal 100 by 2027
- Need: 16,000+ structures
- Cosmic's coverage: 0.6% of need (at 100 homes)
- At their current pace (~35 homes in ~1 year), full rebuild would take ~457 years

### Cost Gap
- Cosmic: $550-700/sqft → a 2,500 sqft home costs $1.375M - $1.75M
- Traditional LA: $800-1000+/sqft → same home costs $2M - $2.5M+
- Savings: $625K - $750K per home
- But many Palisades homes were 3,000-5,000+ sqft → $1.65M - $3.5M even with Cosmic
