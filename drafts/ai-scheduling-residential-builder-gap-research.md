# Research: AI Construction Scheduling — The Residential Builder Gap

## Angle
ALICE Technologies + McKinsey just formalized a commercial alliance for AI-powered "construction optioneering." Their platform simulates millions of construction scenarios and has reduced data center schedules by 40%. But it requires BIM models and Primavera P6 schedules — tools that custom home builders don't use. The residential builder is left managing $500K–$2M projects with Excel, group texts, and whiteboards while $51M+ in VC funding builds AI scheduling for the $100M+ commercial market.

## Journalist
Marcus Washington — workforce/labor angle. The small builder managing 5-10 simultaneous projects, coordinating 15+ subcontractor crews by phone, while AI scheduling tools target commercial GCs running $500M projects.

## Primary Sources

### ALICE Technologies + McKinsey Alliance
- McKinsey and ALICE Technologies formalized commercial alliance for AI scheduling on large capital projects
- Deployed across 35+ clients
- Schedule reductions of up to 20%, one data center case: 40% reduction
- Platform ingests BIM data and Primavera P6 schedules to simulate millions of sequencing/resource-loading combinations
- Zachry Construction: reduced highway project timeline by 28 days using ALICE
- ALICE saves ~$12.1M per $100M project, cuts 128 days on average (Toloka.vc investment announcement)
- Source: constructiondive.com, aecmag.com, ain.ua

### ALICE Funding History
- $8M Series A (Merus Capital, Foundamental, Blackhorn Ventures, Lightspeed)
- $30M Series B (Vanedge Capital, Bouygues, JLL Spark, MetaPlanet)
- $13M extension (Swire Properties, Bridge Bank)
- Total: ~$51M+
- Clients: Bouygues Construction, Skanska — commercial/infrastructure giants
- Source: builtinsf.com, pulse2.com, blog.alicetechnologies.com

### Construction Delay Statistics
- 77% of construction projects are late (Procore/IDC survey, 505 respondents)
- Average project is 70 days late vs. original estimates
- 75% over planned budgets
- 6 budget changes and 5 schedule changes per project average
- 15% average cost increase from changes
- 98% of projects experience cost overruns, delays, or both (McKinsey)
- Only 25% complete within 10% of original deadline (KPMG)
- Source: canada.constructconnect.com, openspace.ai

### US Home Construction Timelines
- Average US home construction: 9.1 months nationally (Census Bureau 2024 Survey of Construction)
- Regional range: 7.8 months (South Atlantic) to 13.7 months (Middle Atlantic)
- Pacific region: 10.8 months average
- Permit-to-start wait: 0.9 months (East North Central) to 2.1 months (Pacific)
- Source: indexbox.io (citing Census Bureau)

### Home Remodeling Budget Overruns
- 37% of remodeling projects exceeded budget (Houzz 2026 U.S. Houzz & Home Study)
- 25% of homeowners didn't set a budget before starting
- 35% chose higher-end materials midproject
- 32% cited unanticipated complexity
- 31% changed scope during construction
- Source: stacker.com

### Construction Scheduling Software Market
- Market size: $4.6B in 2025, projected $9.2B by 2034 (CAGR 8.2%)
- Residential construction: only 28.3% market share
- Commercial: 45.2%, Industrial: 16.8%, Infrastructure: 9.7%
- Small/Medium Enterprises: 31.8% share vs Large Enterprises: 68.2%
- Source: dataintelo.com

### Residential Builder Technology
- 64% of US GCs using cloud-based construction software by mid-2025 (PlanRadar survey)
- BUT: BIM adoption in residential mainly for "subdivision-scale projects and high-volume production homes" — not custom builders
- CoConstruct (now part of Buildertrend) targets custom home builders but lacks AI scheduling
- Clear Estimates: ~$59/month for small contractors "moving off spreadsheets for the first time"
- Source: planradar.com, dataintelo.com, linkedin.com

### Academic Research
- AI scheduling can reduce planning time by 30% (systematic review, ResearchGate)
- UEL study (Frontiers in Built Environment, 2026): reviewed 60 studies, found risk prediction and schedule optimization systems operate in isolation — "Projects generate enormous amounts of warning data every day... but nothing in the schedule actually changes"
- Random Forest Regressor reduced project durations by up to 3.52% (AI-BIM integration study, Springer)
- Source: researchgate.net, techxplore.com, link.springer.com
