# Research: muse-every-door-availability-ledger-2026

**Journalist:** Priya Greenwood (everyday life, accessibility, family use)
**Working headline:** "Every Door Into Muse, and Which Ones Are Actually Open"
**Topic:** A complete, honest ledger of every surface through which an ordinary person can reach Muse — what is live today, what was announced at Connect 2026 without a ship date, what it costs to use each one, and where in the world each one works.

## Kill test
Does this help someone understand what Muse can do, use Muse better, or make an informed decision about trusting an AI agent with real work? **YES.** Nobody has published a single annotated ledger that separates Muse's shipped surfaces from its keynote promises, prices them, and maps them by region. An ordinary person deciding whether to fold Muse into daily life needs exactly this: which door to knock on, what it costs, and whether the door is open yet.

## Ledger (the article's backbone)
**Live today:**
- iOS/Android app + web at muse.ai — the full agent (artifacts, side chats, Goals tab, voice mode, background work). Best for deep projects.
- WhatsApp — chat with Muse like a person; quick asks, reminders, short threads. US (possibly US+Canada); NOT named for Japan/Singapore launches. Was not presented as a reader of your existing chats.
- Voice mode — talk instead of typing; dictation, voice notes, live video chat with animated avatar per some guides (treat "rolled out" claim as unverified; Connect says Realtime Avatar has no release date).
- Muse for Mac app — computer use operates native apps, files, mail, messages, calendar, notes; continues queued tasks while you are away. Available now per Connect.
**Announced, no ship date:** dedicated Muse email address (forward mail for it to act on); Realtime Avatar video chat; glasses wake-word integration; Muse Charm pocket device.
**Pricing (verified Oct-Nov 2026):** Free $0 (usage limit, refreshes); Power $20/mo (500M tokens/wk); Maximum $100/mo (3B tokens/wk). Paid plans raise the allowance only, not features. Weekly allowance covers every surface (phone apps, muse.ai, WhatsApp, Mac). Must be 18+ to subscribe. Meta takes a small transaction fee (unpublished amount).
**Regions:** US, Canada, Japan (from Oct 23), Singapore (from Oct 27). No full country list.

## Limitations to state in the article
- Availability claims ("live now") come from Meta's own announcements via reporting; I have not personally tested each surface.
- Free-tier token figure comes from Zuckerberg's launch statement, not the Help Center; readers should check in-app Usage.
- WhatsApp availability: sources disagree (US vs US+Canada); Japan/Singapore posts do not name it.
- "Coming months" and announced features have slipped before; Meta delayed Muse itself from April 2026 over safety issues (VP Vishal Shah quoted in GuruFocus).
- Pricing pages describe limited testing; figures may change.
